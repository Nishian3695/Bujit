Support

Check the common questions below, or send us a message with one of the forms. Forms don't need a Google account; leave an email if you'd like a reply.

Common questions

A bank says "connection expired" or won't update

Banks occasionally ask you to sign in again. Open Linked Accounts from the menu and tap Reconnect on that bank. If it still fails, report a problem below and include the bank's name.

Google says "Google hasn't verified this app" when I turn on Google Tasks sync

Google's review of Bujit's Tasks access is in progress. Until it's done, tap Advanced, then Go to Bujit, to continue. Bujit only uses its own "Bujit" list in Google Tasks.

How do I move my budget into Bujit?

Fill in the CSV template and use Settings → Import from CSV. The CSV import reference explains every column, with examples.

How do I move Bujit to a new phone?

On the old phone, use Settings → Export backup and choose a passphrase. On the new phone, use Settings → Import backup with the same passphrase. Linked banks need to be reconnected afterwards.

How do I delete my data?

See Delete your data. For anything it doesn't cover, use the contact form below with "Delete my data" as the topic.
